# Findings

Living ledger. One row per finding. IDs are `<UNIT>-<NNN>` (`CH4-001`, `CH5-003`,
`ABS-002`); never reuse an ID, even after waiving. Anchor to `\label{}` > section title +
paragraph index > a short grep-able quote — never line numbers. Details live in the session
record where the finding was raised; the row stays one line.

Statuses: `open` → `fixed` (verified in the text, not just claimed) | `waived` (author
override; add a WAIVERS.md entry if it generalizes) | `disputed` (one appeal, resurfaces at
next full review). `First seen` / `Last touched` are session numbers.
