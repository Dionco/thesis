\section{Observing summary for the Cristofari sample}
\label{app:obs_summary}

Spectral types are taken from SIMBAD. Five stars (Gl~617B, GJ~4063, PM~J09553-2715, GJ~1012, PM~J08402+3127) lack PolarBase optical data and are excluded from the optical analysis; their observation entries are marked with em-dashes.
